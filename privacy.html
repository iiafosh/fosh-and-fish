Privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 (version 1.0)

fosh&fish is a fishing game by afosh. This page explains what it stores and shares, and how to delete it. In short: without an account nothing about you leaves your device, except in the Google Play version, which shows ads you choose to watch and sells optional packs through Google Play.

Playing without an account

Your progress is saved on your own device only.

If you make an account

  • Your email address: to sign in and to send you a code if you forget your password.
  • Your fisher name: shown on the public leaderboard, and next to the shiny fish you catch in the public "latest shinies" list.
  • Your game save (level, fish, boats, Pearls, the packs you bought and so on): so you can play on several devices and keep what you bought. Your level, prestige and total money earned appear on the leaderboard next to your fisher name.
  • Your shiny fish: which numbered shiny fish you own and when you caught them. The server hands out the 3,000 shinies of each species, so it keeps this to make the count fair for everyone.
  • Your password is never stored by the game. The sign-in service keeps only a secure hash of it.

Accounts and saves are kept with Supabase (servers in the EU). Other players can only ever see your fisher name, your leaderboard numbers and your shiny fish.

Purchases (Google Play version)

Supporter Packs and Pearls are sold through Google Play, which handles the payment: the game never sees your card or bank details. The game receives what you bought and a purchase code, and keeps them in your save so you get each purchase exactly once. If you are signed in, the game also tells Google Play a scrambled (hashed) version of your account number, which Google uses to spot fraud.

Ads (Google Play version)

The Google Play version has optional ads: you only see one when you press Watch ad for a reward. The ads come from Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law asks for it (for example in the EU and the UK), Google's consent form asks you first, and you can say no. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. More about this: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The PC and web versions have no ads.

Children

fosh&fish is meant for players aged 13 and up, and we don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you think a child under 13 made an account, write to us (below) and we'll delete it.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Menu → Account → Delete account. That removes your login, your fisher name, your cloud save and your shiny fish right away. If you can't open the game, email support@foshfish.xyz from the address of your account and we'll delete it within 30 days. To clear what's on your device, use Settings → Reset save or uninstall the game.

Updates

The PC version checks for new versions by downloading a small file from this website and GitHub; the Google Play version gets its updates from Google Play. No personal data is sent with those checks.

Questions

Email support@foshfish.xyz, or open an issue on GitHub, and afosh will get back to you.
