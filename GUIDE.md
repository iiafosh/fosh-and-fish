# Virtual Fisher — Player Guide (v0.1 beta)

A cozy fishing game based on the **Virtual Fisher** Discord bot. Cast, catch, sell, upgrade,
travel through 7 biomes and prestige for permanent power.

---

## 1. Getting started

| Do this | How |
|---|---|
| **Cast** | Tap/click the water, press **FISH**, or press **Space / F** |
| **Sell** | Press **SELL** or **S** — sells everything in your hold |
| **Shop** | Tap the **fish shop on the island** (teal roof, golden fish) or the **Shop** button |
| **Menus** | Buttons bottom-right, or keys **1–9**; **G** opens this guide in-game |
| **Close a menu** | **Esc**, the ✕, or tap outside it |

Every cast catches several fish at once. After a cast there's a short **cooldown** (the green bar
filling on the FISH button). The **catch card** (top right) shows what you caught, the XP you got
and any chest or pet you found.

**First 10 minutes:** fish until you have **$500**, sell, buy the **Improved Rod**, then buy the
first levels of **Better Fish** and **Salesman** (Shop → Upgrades) and a few **Worms**.
The **Next:** bar under your level always shows the next rod or boat to save for — tap it.

## 2. Gear

### Rods
Rods set how many fish you catch, which fish bite and your treasure chance. Some rods only work in
certain biomes (a ⚠ appears on the Rod button if yours can't be used where you are). The rod you
equip is the rod your fisher holds. Highlights:

| Rod | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plastic → Improved → Steel → Fiberglass | free → $50K | River only |
| Alloy | $250K | Best River rod for rare fish |
| Lava / Magma | $1M / $10M | River, Volcanic, Ocean |
| Golden | $120M (Lv100) | Treasure rod (13% chests) — use for charm grinding |
| Superium / Infinity | $250M / $1B (Lv100) | Ocean & Sky workhorses |
| Floating, Sky, Meteor, Space, Alien… | Lv250+ | Late game |

### Bait (1 used per cast)
Worms (+2 fish) → **Leeches** (Lv10) → Magnet (Lv20, treasure) → **Wise Bait** (Lv30, +150% XP) →
**Fish** (Lv40) → Artifact Magnet (Lv60, treasure quality) → **Magic Bait** (Lv80, best all-round) →
Support Bait (Lv150, pets). Use bait for its purpose: Wise Bait for XP, Magic Bait for money.

### Boats
Each boat gives **−0.25s cooldown and +1 fish per cast**. They're bought in order
(Rowboat $5K … Abyssal Surveyor). Your current boat is the one you see on the water.

### Upgrades
- **Shop upgrades** (money): Better Fish, Salesman, Bait Efficiency, More Chests, Worker Motivation,
  Artifact Specialist, Experienced, Better Chests, Better Dailies. Reset when you prestige.
- **Special upgrades** (exotic fish, Lv50+): Fish Ovens, Bait Lover, Aquatic Expert, Boost Booster,
  Statistician, Duplicator, Charmer… Reset when you prestige.
- **League upgrades** (Hooks): never reset. Buy **Super Crates** first.

## 3. Progress

### Levels & biomes
XP from fish levels you up; every level-up pays money. Travel with the **Map** button:

| Biome | Level | Cooldown | Catch rate | Fish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| River | 1 | 3.5s base | 100% | Raw Fish → Pufferfish |
| Volcanic | 50 | +0.5s | 60% | Fiery Pufferfish, Hot Cod |
| Ocean | 100 | +1s | 30% | Squid, Turtle, Dolphin |
| Sky | 250 | +2s | 12% | Guardian, Emerald Squid, Rainbow Fish |
| Space | 500 | +3s | 6.5% | Space Fish, Galactic Crab |
| Alien | 1,000 | +4s | 3% | Shark, Alien Fish |
| Abyss | 2,500 | +5s | 1% | Artifact Fish, Dark Puffer, Dark Tuna |

Higher biomes give fewer fish per cast but each is worth far more.

### Chests, exotic fish & charms
- From **level 10** casts can find **chests**: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Artifact
  (and Super with the league upgrade). Better rods/bait/upgrades raise treasure chance and quality.
- Chests give money, XP and **exotic fish**: Gold & Emerald (Lv10), Lava (Lv50), Diamond (Lv100).
  Tap the **wallet** next to your money to see them.
- From **level 20** Rare+ chests drop **charms** (8 types). Tier *n* of a charm costs *n* charms.
  You need **all charms maxed (440 at P0)** to prestige.

### Pets
Pets **can't be bought** — each cast has a **1 in 10,000** chance (more with Support Bait and at
higher prestige). The equipped pet gains XP as you fish. Puffer = fish catch, Zebrafish = fish quality,
Dolphin = XP + treasure, Axolotl = treasure quality, Tuna = a bit of everything.

### Boosts, workers, quests & daily
- **Boosts** (Lv10, Boosts menu): Fish boost, Treasure boost and **Workers** that fish for you.
  Gold fish = short boosts, Emerald fish = long ones. Save Gold fish for Statistician later.
- **Quests** reset daily: level-ups, fish caught and chests found pay money and XP.
  The **league quest** pays **10 Hooks**, and every 500 trips in a week pays 10 more.
- **Daily reward** (Lv10): claim once a day; keep the streak (within 48h) for bigger rewards.

## 4. Prestige

At **Level 250 + 440/440 charms + $5B** open **Prestige → Prestige**. You restart from level 1 but keep
**pets, Hooks, league upgrades and prestige perks**, and get **1 Azure Fish**.

Spend Azure Fish in **Prestige → Shop** (cap = 1 + prestige/5):
International Ties (+10% everything), Business Education (+40% sell), Fish Whisperer (+25% catch),
Ancient One (+35% XP), Virtual Fisher (P5+, quality/sell/XP). **Prestige → Guide** shows the
community buy order for P1–P160 (your current prestige is highlighted). First prestige: buy
**International Ties**.

---

## 5. Tips (from the Virtual Fisher Prestige 0 Guide)
- Always fish with bait (except on the Plastic Rod — rush the Improved Rod first).
- Buy boats in order — they all give the same buff.
- Don't buy upgrades that cost more than ~5% of your next rod.
- Fish in the highest biome your rod allows for money and XP.
- Start the charm grind early (Golden Rod + Artifact Magnet in the Ocean) — charms are needed to
  prestige and boost everything.
- The **Buffs** menu shows your exact multipliers and the catch odds for your rod in this biome.

## 6. Platforms & saves
- **Windows:** unzip and run `VirtualFisher.exe` (no install needed). If Windows SmartScreen warns, click *More info → Run anyway* (the beta isn't code-signed).
- **Linux:** extract the `.tar.gz` and run `./VirtualFisher.x86_64`.
- **Android:** copy `VirtualFisher-0.1-beta-android.apk` to the phone, open it and allow *Install unknown apps* when asked. Android 7.0 or newer.
- **Browser:** open the web version; on iPhone/iPad use it in Safari and *Add to Home Screen*.
- The game **autosaves** every 20 seconds and when you close it. Each device/browser has its own save.
  **Buffs → Reset save…** wipes progress (asks twice).
