# fosh&fish roadmap

## Next version (after 0.5)

Built from an honest "why would a gamer quit?" review of 0.5, plus tester feedback
(send yours in-game: **G → Feedback**, or [open an issue](https://github.com/iiafosh/fosh-and-fish/issues/new/choose)).

### Why players might quit today, and the fix
| Problem | Plan |
|---|---|
| No skill: the loop is tap, wait, sell | Catch mini-game: choose **Relax** (wait for the cooldown) or **Reel it in!** (win = +25% fish and a 0.5 s cooldown), funny and snappy |
| No music, only water sounds | Original background music per biome |
| No "wow" moments after the first hour | Legendary / boss fish in every biome, events, rare surprises |
| The biomes are static | Things to discover while sailing |
| The fisherman just stands at the front of the boat | A funnier fisherman who sits on deck, yawns, sips a drink, dances on big catches |

### Open feedback and known issues
- Web: a rare black screen seen once in automated testing; to verify on real phone browsers.
- Android: test the APK on real phones; a release signing key is needed before any Play Store release.
- The new Sailboat looks bulky from above.
- Accounts: email confirmation needs an email service (or "Confirm email" off) before many players sign up.
- Ideas for later: share your catch to TikTok, "while you were away" catches.
