== Overview ==
Boats are upgrades that reduce your fish cooldown and give you slightly more fishes. Each boat give the same buff of -0.25s fishing cooldown and +1 fish per cast. Boats can be purchased with cash and have a level requirement corresponding to the biomes you have unlocked.

Note: the +1 fish is NOT subject to modification by baithelper and baitlover, but is scaled by other factors in the fish catch formula e.g. biome reducer, duplicator, boosters.
