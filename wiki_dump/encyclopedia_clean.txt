              Treasure Quality +10.75% (Lv 1) +85.00% (Lv 100) 103.75% (Lv 125)

NOTE: It is not possible to get duplicate pets before owning all 5. Once you have all 5 pets, any further pets you get will convert it into xp for that pet type.
Pets are more common to get if you don’t get one within a certain amount of trips. They are 1.5x as common if no pets have been found in 10k trips, and 3x as common if not found in 15k trips. This only applies to the first pet you get.

Upgrades
Upgrades. One of the most important things in progressing. Giving yourself little stat boosts to make life (and fishing) easier. Apart of the bot there are three different categories of upgrades. All with different currencies required to purchase, Normal Upgrades, costing money. Special Upgrades, costing Exotic Fish, and Prestige Upgrades, bought with Azure Fish.

Normal Upgrades
You can access these by running the command “/shop upgrades”. These 9 upgrades cost money, which as you should know by now, you can get from selling fish, from treasures, or as a daily or vote reward.

Better Fish - Increases Fish Quality by 5%. Can be upgraded 21 times, and costs $14,902,000 to max.

Salesman - Increases sell price by 5% per upgrade. Can be upgraded 18 times, and costs $4,058,000 to max.

Bait Efficiency - Lowers the chance of consuming bait by 5% when casting. Can be upgraded 9 times, and costs $220,500 to max.

More Chests - Increases Treasure Chance by 5%. Can be upgraded 11 times, and costs $6,087,000 to max.

Worker Motivation - Increases Fish Catch from workers by 10%. Can be upgraded 12 times, and costs $777,310,000 to max.

Artifact Specialist - Improves Treasure rewards by 10% excluding charms. Can be upgraded 7 times, and costs $521,500 to max.

Experienced - Increases XP Gain by 10%. Can be upgraded 5 times, and costs $555,550,000 to max.

Better Chests - Increases Treasure Quality by 10%. Can be upgraded 5 times, and costs $131,100,000 to max.

Better Dailies - Increases Daily Rewards items by 10%. Can be upgraded 10 times, and costs $2,990,000 to max.

Special Upgrades
These upgrades can be accessed by running the command “/shop special”. These take three different kinds of exotic fish to be able to upgrade. Lava Fish, Diamond Fish and Gold Fish.

Fish Ovens - Increases Sell Price by 5%. Can be upgraded 20 times, and costs 3360 Lava Fish to max.

Bait Lover - Increases effectiveness of bait by 15%. Can be upgraded 4 times, and costs 385 Diamond Fish to max.

Aquatic Expert - Increases Fish Catch by 5%. Can be upgraded 4 times, and costs 385 Diamond Fish to max.

Worker Extender - Increases the length of Worker Boosts by 10%. Can be upgraded 4 times, and costs 385 Diamond Fish to max.

Ultimate Salesman - Increases Sell Price by 15%. Can be upgraded 4 times, and costs 385 Diamond Fish to max.

Highly Experienced - Increases XP Gain by 15%. Can be upgraded 4 times, and costs 395 Diamond Fish to max.

Boost Booster - Increases Treasure Quality by 50% (Extra 10% per Level),  Fish Quality by 40% (Extra 2% per Level), and Worker fish speed by 0.1[1]. (By default, they fish every 20s.). Can be Upgraded 4 times, and costs 385 Diamond Fish to max.

Statistician - Increases all Multipliers by 2%. Can be upgraded 10 times, and costs 4000 Gold Fish to max.

Duplicator - Increases chance to duplicate fish by 2%. This only affects your current trip when it procs. Can be upgraded 10 times, and costs 4400 Emerald Fish to max.

Charmer - Increases Charms found by 2.5%. Can be upgraded 10 times, and costs 3200 Lava Fish to max.

Fish Whisperer - Increases Fish Catch by 25%. Can be upgraded 26 times on max prestige. A new tier unlocks every 5 prestiges, and costs 1 Azure Fish.

Ancient One - Increases XP Gain by 35%. Can be upgraded 26 times on max prestige. A new tier unlocks every 5 prestiges, and costs 1 Azure Fish.

Virtual Fisher - Increases Fish Quality by 40%, Sell Price by 20%, and XP Gain by 20%. This upgrade unlocks upon reaching Prestige 5. Can be upgraded 26 times on max prestige. A new tier unlocks every 5 prestiges, and costs 1 Azure Fish.


League Upgrades
These upgrades are available as soon as you start the bot. You can earn currency for these upgrades by completing daily special quests, which reward 10 hooks upon completion, or by hitting a certain milestone of trips in a week, with a max reward of 100 hooks. The maximum amount of hooks you can earn in a week is 170. A total of 6435 hooks is needed to max every upgrade. Upgrades bought in this shop are permanent.

Pet Helper - Increases max Pet Level by 5, and increases the experience of your lowest level pet by a small margin. Can be upgraded 5 times, and costs 1050 hooks to max. 

Bait Helper - Increases effectiveness of Bait by 5%. Can be upgraded 5 times, and costs 1050 hooks to max.

Super Crates - Unlocks the ability to find Super Crates. Level 1 chance is 1/200. Can be upgraded 5 times, and costs 1050 hooks to max.

Worker Crates - Unlocks the ability for Workers to fish crates. With no buffs on the worker, it has a 1/50 chance to find a crate. Can be upgraded 5 times, and costs 1050 hooks to max.
[1]Workers by default fish every 20 seconds. Any upgrade of Fishing Frenzy, or Boost booster, increases the speed of workers by 10%. With a level 2 Boost Booster upgrade, and a level 1 Fishing Frenzy upgrade, workers fish 30% faster (1 trip every ~15.38s). The fastest the worker can fish with max upgrades is ~10.52s.
**The chances for a Worker to fish a crate for each level are: Level 1 - 1/50 | Level 2 - 1/40 | Level 3 - 1/32 | Level 4 - 1/26 | Level 5 - 1/20. Treasure Quality affects the rewards as well.

Fishing Frenzy - Increases Worker fish speed by 10% per upgrade. Increases Fish Boost’s effectiveness by 1%. Can be upgraded 5 times, and costs 1050 hooks to max.
While the Fish Boost may seem like a small increase, it can vastly change the rewards that you can get while using the Fish Boost in the Boosts shop. The formula for the Fish Boost was changed with the addition to the leagues shop making it: 5% fish increase + 1% per Boost Booster and Fishing Frenzy level + (0-3 fish * Fishing Frenzy level + 1-4 fish + Boost Booster level) * Rod Multiplier * Biome Multiplier.

Duplicator 2.0 - Increases Duplication chance by 2%. Can be upgraded 10 times, and costs 1185 hooks to max.
