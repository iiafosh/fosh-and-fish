= '''Overview''' =
'''Upgrades''' are buffs you can purchase for ingame currency and provide long-lasting buffs. Most upgrades last for a prestige and sometimes permanently. Upgrades can usually be purchased via the /shop <category> command and some upgrade type can be purchased in bulk such as shop upgrades, special upgrades, ....

Note: All upgrades stack with itself additively (for example, buying 5% fish catch buff clan upgrade twice would give you 10% more fish catch or 1.1x fish catch multiplier instead of (1.05x)^2 fish catch multiplier
 
= '''List and Description of Upgrades''' =
== '''Shop Upgrades''' ==
Shop Upgrade are upgrades that can be purchased through the regular shop using the /shop upgrades command. They can be bought with in-game cash and get reset with each prestige. The upgrades overall are relatively cheap and have no requirement to unlock, as such the shop can be maxed out quite early on.

== Prestige Upgrades  ==
'''Prestige Upgrades''' are upgrades that can be purchased in the prestige shop (using the command /shop prestige). The prestige shop start out at a cap of /1 per upgrade, and every 5 prestiges the cap increase by +1 (so P5 the cap will be /2, P10 the cap will be /3, .... The formula for the prestige upgrade cap is '''1 + [p/5] (where p is the prestige count)'''). Each upgrade cost 1 azure fish to purchase and will last your entire game.

==== '''List of Prestige Upgrades''' ====
<div class="mw-collapsible> <div class="mw-collapsible-content">

'''Business Education''' - Increases Sell Price by 40%.

'''Fish Whisperer''' - Increases Fish Catch by 25%.

'''Ancient One''' - Increases XP Gain by 35%.

'''Virtual Fisher''' [Unlock at prestige 5]- Increases Fish Quality by 40%, Sell Price by 20%, and XP Gain by 20%.

== '''Clan Upgrades'''  ==

'''Clan Upgrades''' are upgrades that can be purchased in the clan shop. They provide buffs to your clan or to your clan members including yourself. Each upgrade cost 1 clan point and you gain 1 clan point per 5 clan levels.  

==== '''List of Clan Upgrades''' ====
<div class="mw-collapsible> <div class="mw-collapsible-content">

'''Extra Member:''' +1 clan member, can be purchased 5 times.

'''10% Sell Price Buff:''' +10% Sell Price, can be purchased 2 times.

'''5% Fish Catch Buff:''' +5% Fish Catch, can be purchased 2 times.

'''5% XP Buff:''' +5% XP, can be purchased 3 times.

</div> </div>

-The upgrade cap of different type of upgrades has been increased in multiple instances throughout the bot history. Shop upgrades have their cap increased in 1.4.1 while for diamond fish it's update 1.9 [[File:Screenshot 2026-09-28 223023.jpg|thumb|A screenshot of the shop upgrades before higher cap]].
