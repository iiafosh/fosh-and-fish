== '''Overview''' ==
Biomes are unlocked as you progress further through the bot once you reach a certain levels. Each unlocked at a different level, and rewarding different types of fish per cast as well as new upgrades, exotic fishes, and boat. The biomes will impact your casting time by a little, which can be lowered again with the available boats to purchase. There are a total of 7 biomes as of now.

[[File:Screenshot 3.png|thumb|A list of biomes when using the /biome command. This command was done in version 1.19, before abyss biome was added]]

Higher biomes also give new exotic fishes to unlock upgrades (Gold/Emerald fishes are unlock in river biomes or above. Lava fishes are unlocked in volcanic biomes or above, diamond fishes are unlocked in ocean biomes or above). Biomes also boost pet xp by an some amount.

You can switch to different biomes or view your current biomes using the /biome command.

== Biomes Requirement ==
You unlock biomes by levelling up. The level requirement to unlock the biomes are listed below :

'''River''' - Unlocked right away (level 1)

'''Volcanic''' - Level 50

'''Ocean''' - Level 100

'''Sky''' - Level 250

'''Space''' - Level 500

'''Alien''' - Level 1000

'''Abyss''' - Level 2500
