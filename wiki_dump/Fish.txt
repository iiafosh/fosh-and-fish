'''Overall'''

Fish (not to be confused with exotic fishes), are currencies you get by doing fishing trips. They grant you xp upon catching and stay in your inventory which you can later on sell for money. Different fishes can be unlocked based on your biome and fish quality. 

Fish can be buffed by these following buffs:
- Sell Price (Increase value when selling fish)
-Fish Catch (Increase amount of fish per trip)
-Fish Quality (Increase your chance of getting higher rarity fishes)
-XP Multiplier (Increase the amount of XP fish gives)
