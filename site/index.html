    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No ads, no purchases. fosh&fish is a hobby project by afosh.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Your progress is saved on your device. A free account adds cloud saves (play on your phone and your PC with the same fish) and a spot on the leaderboard.

    How do updates work?

    Download once. When there's a new version the game tells you on its title screen. Press Update and it installs in seconds, with your progress kept. The browser version is always up to date.

    Windows says it protected my PC. Is the game safe?

    That warning appears for new games that aren't code-signed yet. Choose More info → Run anyway. The game's code is open on GitHub, and every update is signed and checked by the game before it installs.

    I found a bug / I have an idea

    Great! Use Guide → Feedback in the game, or open an issue on GitHub.
